🔄 Confusing Words

Accept / Except

Accept: स्वीकार करना — Please accept my invitation.

कृपया मेरा निमंत्रण स्वीकार करें।

Except: को छोड़कर — Everyone came except Rahul.

राहुल को छोड़कर सभी आए।

Advice / Advise

Advice: सलाह — My advice is useful.

Advise: सलाह देना — I advise you to practise daily.

Affect / Effect

Affect: प्रभावित करना — Lack of sleep can affect health.

Effect: प्रभाव — The effect was positive.

Since / For

I have been studying for two hours. — मैं दो घंटे से पढ़ रहा हूँ।

I have been studying since 6 p.m. — मैं शाम 6 बजे से पढ़ रहा हूँ।

📝 HAS / HAVE — Complete Guide

HAS

He / She / It + has — possession, relationships, conditions and Present Perfect.

उसके पास एक कार है। → She has a car.

उसने काम पूरा कर लिया है। → She has finished the work.

HAVE

I / You / We / They + have.

मेरे पास एक किताब है। → I have a book.

उन्होंने काम पूरा कर लिया है। → They have finished the work.

🚫 HAS NOT / HAVE NOT

has not / hasn't → He, She, It

have not / haven't → I, You, We, They

उसने अभी काम पूरा नहीं किया है। → She has not finished the work yet.

उन्होंने अभी तक खाना नहीं खाया है। → They have not eaten yet.

Important: In Present Perfect, use has/have + Verb की 3rd form, not has/have + Verb की 2nd form.

💰 Haves and Have-nots

The haves means people who have sufficient wealth, resources or advantages.

The have-nots means people who lack sufficient wealth, resources or advantages.

There can be a gap between the haves and have-nots.

साधन-संपन्न लोगों और साधन-विहीन लोगों के बीच अंतर हो सकता है।

⏳ SINCE / FOR — Special Focus

SINCE = starting point
since Monday • since 2020 • since morning • since 6 p.m.
FOR = duration / period
for two hours • for five years • for three days • for a month

Present Perfect Continuous

Subject + has/have been + V-ing + since/for

I have been studying since morning. — मैं सुबह से पढ़ रहा/रही हूँ।

I have been studying for two hours. — मैं दो घंटे से पढ़ रहा/रही हूँ।

She has been working since 9 a.m. — वह सुबह 9 बजे से काम कर रही है।

She has been working for three hours. — वह तीन घंटे से काम कर रही है।

पहचान: कब से शुरू हुआ? → Since. कितनी अवधि से? → For.
